# Context.ai

> Context (context.ai) is a unified enterprise AI platform for building, deploying, and improving AI agents at scale. Modular components: Workspace (plain-English agent workflow authoring), Engine (800+ connectors with IdP-brokered identity), Unify (institutional-knowledge repository), and Evals (rubric-based quality scoring). Model-agnostic (Claude, GPT, Gemini, Kimi, open weights) with hosted, VPC, on-prem, or air-gapped deployment. No public developer/API surface is published at this time.
